CleanTab
What it does

It reads the page,
not your history.

CleanTab scores the text and links of pages you open. When a page looks explicit, it swaps the tab for a pause screen and asks what's actually going on.

Worth knowing before you start

  • It runs entirely on your device. No account, no server, no analytics. Nothing leaves your machine.
  • It will get things wrong. Keyword scoring misfires on health, art and news writing. Every block has a one-click way through, and you can trust a site permanently.
  • Optional image scanning. If you turn it on, pictures you linger on in feeds get classified locally. It costs battery, so it starts off.

Incognito

The window that
needs it most.

Chrome disables every extension in incognito by default — including this one. Until you allow it, an incognito window has no protection at all.

Two steps, and this screen will confirm it worked

  1. Open the extension's settings page with the button below.
  2. Turn on Allow in Incognito, then come back to this tab.
Not allowed in incognito yet.

CleanTab uses split incognito, so an incognito session keeps its own pauses and never writes incognito browsing into your history or your ring.
