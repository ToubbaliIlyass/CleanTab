What it does

It reads the page,
not your history.

CleanTab scores the text, links and images of pages you open. When something looks explicit, it swaps the tab for a pause screen and asks what's actually going on.

  • Entirely on your device. No account, no server, no analytics.
  • It will get things wrong. Every block has a one-click way through.
  • Takes about a minute. Five questions, then you're done.
